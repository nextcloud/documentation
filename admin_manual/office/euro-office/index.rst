================
Nextcloud Office
================

Nextcloud Office is Nextcloud's built-in office solution, powered by the Nextcloud Office Document Server.
It enables real-time collaborative editing of text documents, spreadsheets, and presentations
directly in the browser, with high-fidelity rendering that preserves the layout and formatting
of your files.

Nextcloud Office supports a wide range of formats including DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, ODS, ODP
and many more.

.. note::
    A dedicated Document Server is required for production use. Install the server first, then
    connect Nextcloud to it using the
    `Nextcloud Office app <https://apps.nextcloud.com/apps/eurooffice>`_.

The Nextcloud Office Document Server is built from `Euro-Office <https://github.com/Euro-Office>`_, an open
source office suite, and packaged and supported by Nextcloud.
