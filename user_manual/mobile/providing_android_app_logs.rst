==========================
Providing Android app logs
==========================

When you report an issue with the Nextcloud Android app, attach the app logs right away.
Logs help the developers find and fix the cause of the issue faster.

Sending logs from the app
-------------------------

.. note::

   The screenshots below were taken on Android 16 with the Nextcloud Android app 3.35. Menus and options may look
   slightly different depending on your Android version and app version.

.. important::

   You must be logged in to an account in the app to access the logs.

1. Open the Nextcloud Android app, open the navigation drawer and tap **Settings**.

   .. figure:: images/providing-android-app-logs-1.png
      :alt: Navigation drawer of the Nextcloud Android app with the Settings entry.
      :width: 300px

      *Figure 1: The navigation drawer with the Settings entry.*

2. In the **More** section, tap **Logs**.

   .. figure:: images/providing-android-app-logs-2.png
      :alt: Settings screen of the Nextcloud Android app with the Logs entry.
      :width: 300px

      *Figure 2: The Logs entry in the settings.*

3. Optionally, enter a search term in the toolbar to filter the log entries.

   .. figure:: images/providing-android-app-logs-3.png
      :alt: Log screen filtered by the search term Upload.
      :width: 300px

      *Figure 3: Log entries filtered by a search term.*

4. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner and select **Send logs by email**.

   .. figure:: images/providing-android-app-logs-4.png
      :alt: Three-dot menu of the log screen with the Send logs by email option.
      :width: 300px

      *Figure 4: The Send logs by email option.*

5. Select the app to send the logs with, for example your email app.
